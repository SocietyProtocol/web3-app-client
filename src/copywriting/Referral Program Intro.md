# Referral Program

Grow the network by inviting trusted members. Every accepted invitation strengthens the Society Protocol community and unlocks rewards for both you and the people you bring in.

## How it works

The referral program connects you directly to the people you invite, forming a verifiable on-chain relationship between profiles. When you share your referral code with someone new, you vouch for them — and once they accept your invitation, the connection becomes part of your shared network history.

Each profile can generate referral codes tied to its associated wallet address. New users entering the network with a valid code complete their onboarding faster and become formally affiliated with the referrer. Affiliations are recorded on-chain and surface across the protocol: in your profile, in community memberships, and in governance contexts where trust networks matter.

The program is open to all profile holders. There is no limit on how many people you can invite, and accepting a referral does not commit you to anything beyond joining the network — you remain free to participate in any community or hold any badge.

## Generate a referral code

Paste the wallet address of the person you want to invite into the **Generate a Referral Code** field below, then click **Generate**. Share the resulting code with the recipient through any channel. Codes expire 24 hours after they're issued — if your invitee can't accept in time, generate a fresh one.

## Accept an invitation

If someone sent you a referral code, paste it into the **Enter Referral Code to Accept Invitation** field below and click **Accept Invitation**. The invitation is processed on-chain and your affiliation becomes active immediately.
