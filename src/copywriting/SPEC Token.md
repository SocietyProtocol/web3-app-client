#### The Purpose

The purpose of the SPEC token is to store a value of contribution to Society Protocol. This value is retrieved upon the launch of the Society Protocol's minimum-viable-product (MVP) and thereafter, for the length of the duration that it takes humanity to transition the world into Synchronized States, as communities launch new Web4 instances.

#### How it works
The SPEC token is designed to measure (objective) contributions to the Society Protocol (SP) movement. It works in conjunction with the account system, which is designed to measure (subjective) contributions. Together the two systems combine to provide our movement with a real-time sybil-resistant identity map of all participants, their contributions, and their characteristics.

This sybil-resistant community map forms the backbone of allowing anyone to launch their unique SP Web4 instances, by giving them a legitimate map of sybil-resistant accounts on Ethereum, consisting of our movement's participants classified by their subjective (accounts) and objective (SPEC) contribution. Everyone launching an instance of Society Protocol will have a choice to make––_"who gets to be the initial Genesis Actors in my instance?"_ and _"how much Energy does each one of them receive?"_ Everyone will approach this problem differently, but our hypothesis is: that providing a list of mapped accounts...will be used to easily launch SP instances, which are filled with legitimate and interested participants, immediately, at scale, in an accessible manner (by mapping Ethereum accounts to the SP account system).

Each community launching a Society Protocol instance will choose for themselves how to allocate Genesis Actor roles and Energy. Here are some examples:

- Let's say that I only want a society of builders...so I invite only those accounts with the builder badge and allocate Energy to each account based on their amount of SPEC tokens.
- I want only a community of Argentines, and I want a relatively equal community...so I decide to only invite those accounts who are shown to be from Argentina and have >X SPEC tokens (for sybil-resistance) as Genesis Actors...all Energy is equally distributed at the start between those accounts.

Additionally, some of our community partners will make their intentions clear as pledges to use the account systems and SPEC tokens in some manner when they launch SP instances.

#### SPEC Use Cases

The primary use case of SPEC is always going to be held as stored value and recognized by communities launching SP instances to allocate Energy to legitimate participants. That process will last up to ~75 years. The Web3 Outpost will remain standing, valuable, and necessary to facilitate the launching of Web4 SP instances for this entire duration...until, essentially the entire world is converted into Synchronized States.

There are additionally alternative use cases for SPEC present in the Web3 world, as we make this journey, and we're discovering more of them. At launch, there will be two alternative SPEC use cases:

- **VIP Rooms (Gold, Silver, Bronze)** – Community members can lock up SPEC tokens to enter into exclusive VIP rooms where other members are just as committed to this journey towards Synchronized States (Network States, Web3, Web4), and will be together on this journey for the next ~75 years––allowing these communities to form valuable relationships and lucrative partnerships.
- **Liquidity Provision** – 5% of all SPEC tokens are allocated as rewards for users who provide liquidity during the period leading up to the MVP release. Users can lock-up their SPEC tokens to receive a portion of these rewards.

#### SPEC Role in Governance

The SPEC token is not directly involved in governance. There are three things to be noted here:

1. Governors may potentially include SPEC holdings in their considerations for making decisions.
2. The SPEC token can be used as feedback for gauging community support of governance decisions (SPEC tokenholders can vote alongside the Governors, as a feedback mechanism).
3. In the future, there are plans to implement modular decision-making, where SPEC tokenholders may be allocated decision-making power on a per-decision basis by the governors.

#### Token Distribution

The SPEC token will have a total supply of 10,000,000,000 tokens. It is allocated to various parts and contributors in our movement as displayed in the graphic above.

- **Affiliates (25%)** – This portion of SPEC tokens is allocated for establishing mutually beneficial partnerships with our partner communities and advisors. It serves as a way to reward their valuable contributions and to align interests.
- **Core Team (7%)** – The core team allocation is there to better align interests inside of the core team, as we work on the project. It's rewarded to core team members in addition to salaries. This segment is relatively small because our core team should make-up a relatively small portion of our movement.
- **Treasury (27%)** – The treasury of the movement is governed by the DAO governors and initially retains 27% of the SPEC tokens, allocated for future funding endeavors (to keep the project sustainable for the next parts of the journey, after the MVP is released).
- **ICO (31%)** – The most significant portion of the token supply is allocated to the public fundraiser ICO, which will only happen once and last up to six months. It's designed to raise capital and form a committed core to our movement from a wide swathe of worldwide participants––creating a committed serious community with skin in the game.
- **Liquidity Providers (5%)** – This portion is designed to incentivize participants for providing SPEC liquidity. 5% of the total token supply will be placed as rewards into a liquidity pool consisting of SPEC/WETH and distributed evenly to its pool of liquidity providers over a period of five years.
- **Retroactive Rewards (5%)** – These are tokens earmarked from the treasury for rewarding valuable contributions from our community "after-the-fact" with SPEC. It will be up to the governors to decide how to allocate these rewards to the community.
