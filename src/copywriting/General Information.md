# General Information

Welcome to the Web3 Outpost application!

The purpose of the Web3 Outpost is to create the space for our movement to make the transition from Web3 to Web4 possible.
This application provides us with some essential features and a censorship-resistant Schelling point to keep our movement aligned and centered while on this journey.

We expect the Web3 Outpost to remain standing a long time...and _evolve_! As time marches on, the Web3 Outpost will be updated with new features to better help us bridge the world into Synchronized States.

> A lot of the information presented on this page can be more fully understood by reading the _How We Are Building Society Protocol_ article (05 🌐 Society Protocol/Content Library/Published/Articles), which details all pieces of our grand plans for the future.

## Features

The Web3 Outpost application is foundational in transitioning the world from Nation States/Web3 into Synchronized States/Web4.
The features it contains to accomplish this are:

- **Accounts**: A sybil-resistant account system, accessible to any user in our movement, built on top of Ethereum.
- **Badges**: Badges are a way to track <u>contributions</u> and <u>characteristics</u>, distinguishing accounts.
- **Communities**: Allows the various communities participating in the Society Protocol (SP) movement to separate themselves into their own functional enclaves.
- **Governance**: The governance systems allow us to make decisions as a movement––both in the official SP DAO, and for communities to make their own separate decisions in their enclaves.
- **SPEC Token**: The SPEC token allows us to store value contributed to the movement in an objective metric, with aligned incentives, to be recouped later.
- **ICO Fundraise**: The initial coin offering (ICO) fundraise allows our movement to raise capital in order to pursue the building of Society Protocol.

#### Accounts

All users are welcome to create an account when they join the Society Protocol movement. The account is meant to stay with you over time.
As we move forward on this journey, each account will accumulate a unique history––consisting of badges and SPEC tokens––showing its unique identity.
These same identities will later be used to transition into Society Protocol instances, starting when the MVP is developed (3-6 years).

The account system will remain active for many decades. After the MVP is completed, many new Web4 SP instances will spawn from the Web3 account system every year.
The account system helps all communities and participants who are part of the SP movement establish a singular history...so that all participants and communities can transition directly into SP instances with their accumulated histories fully intact.

Accounts have a referral system, which they can use to create a permanent relationship chain between their accounts. Later on, this chain can be seen and used to create SP instances in a way that acknowledges these relationships. (The referral feature is meant to imitate an infantile version of the Parenting Value Function (VF).)

Participants can create as many accounts as they desire, but we suggest only one account per person––allowing it to accumulate a real history of identity over time via badges––which will then transition together with its <u>contributions</u> and <u>characteristics</u> into SP instances.

#### Badges

Badges are NFTs attached to accounts, they are flexible by design—serving either as functional credentials that unlock specific permissions, or as permanent attributions that create a verifiable record of an account's <u>contributions</u> and <u>characteristics</u>. Badges are what differentiates accounts over time.

Anyone is able and welcome to create badges. When the Society Protocol DAO creates a badge, it is considered an "official" badge. When a community creates a badge, it is considered a badge of that community. Each badge is differentiated by: who created it, what it signifies, and what it controls.

Badges can support a wide range of qualities...including verifiable qualities, by using data from smart contracts and zkTLS. Some examples include:

- Communities can (manually) award badges to those who have attended their in-person conferences or pop-up cities
- Badges can be used to verify important information such as countries of origin, financial records, and age (by using zkTLS)
- Communities can award badges to those who have bought and used an ENS name specifically from their domain (via smart contracts)
- Badges can be used to verify social media accounts of influencers with a certain unique follower, amount of followers, or created before a certain date (by using zkTLS)

All of these badges can then be used by communities to grant special privileges to accounts, such as: discounts on network state residency, access to exclusive token gated chat channels, allowing entry only for individuals from a specific country of origin, moderator access or status hierarchy signifiers within their communities, etc...

Much of the value of badges comes from communities that intend to launch Synchronized State instances and award badges to segment their intended audience in meaningful ways. We encourage our movement's participants to identify characteristics they believe will be desirable account qualities in their Synchronized State instances, and create badges for those <u>contributions</u> or <u>characteristics</u>––awarding them to appropriate accounts.

Badges are represented by ERC-1155 NFTs on Ethereum.
Each badge has four permissions: **mint** (who can mint this badge), **burn** (who can burn this badge), **transfer** (who can transfer this badge), and **manage** (who can edit this badge).

**Official Badges:** Here's a list of initial official badges upon release from the SP DAO:

- **Account Badge** – Each account receives an account badge. It handles the profile information of each account––consisting of an avatar, a profile bio, and more structured/unstructured fields which can be defined in the future to display other characteristics, such as age, country, or interests.
- **Governor Badge** – The official Society Protocol DAO governance is a democracy of accounts holding governor badges. Accounts may possess multiple governor badges (representing their total voting power).
- **VIP Room Badges (Gold, Silver, Bronze)** – The VIP Room badges grant accounts access to exclusive channels––with a dedicated exclusive crowd, devoid of spam, and a high level of quality conversations. They can be obtained by locking up the SPEC tokens for a period of time in order to obtain the VIP Room badge.
- **Partner Community Badge (Gold, Silver, Bronze)** – Awarded to official partner communities of the Society Protocol movement. They come in three tiers (Gold, Silver, and Bronze). These badges signify affiliation and ideological alignment. They grant many privileges to our partner communities.
- **Advisor Badge** – Awarded to our advisors to identify them and recognize their status and contributions.
- **Core Team Badge** – Awarded to our core team members (after 6 months) to identify them and recognize their status and contributions.
- **Contributor Badge** – Awarded to individuals who have contributed in various ways: research on our forums, designs, illustrations, art, architecture, or code to the SP codebase to recognize their status and contributions.
- **ICO Participant Badge** – Awarded to all accounts that participate in the ICO fundraiser event to recognize their status and contributions.
- **Moderator Badge** – Awarded to individuals who moderate our communication channels and forums to recognize their status and contributions.

#### Future Plans

- **Communities** – We're actively developing a section to let communities form in our interface. This is for communities who would like to start SP instances as a unified community. These communities will be able to incorporate accounts, create unique badges specifically for their community, share a treasury, and govern themselves as a group––just like the main Society Protocol community.

- **Comms tools** – We are looking at the potential for extending our Web3 Outpost's account system to facilitate communications: encrypted P2P messaging, chat rooms, social media, community forums, etc...all using the same censorship-resistant account infrastructure––based on our Web3 Outpost's account system.

- **Connecting accounts between the Web3 application, Web3 forums, and comms tools** – This feature can combine the entire SP ecosystem into one unified account system––fueling sybil-resistant and censorship-resistant human identity and communications rooted in Web3––while building the future of humanity past that with Society Protocol and the transition to Web4.

- **NFT Platform** – In the TEAA, avatars will identify individual entities and flags will identify organizations in Synchronized States. All our Web3 Outpost accounts are able to upload avatars in their account badge. We are considering producing an NFT platform, where users can create and take to market their own unique blends and collections of avatars and flags, to differentiate themselves and their communities.
