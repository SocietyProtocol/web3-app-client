The ICO will come alive after we've raised sufficient awareness around the project.

We're aiming to move fast, and expecting to launch the ICO around ~October 1st, 2026.

The ICO will sell 31% of the entire SPEC token supply.

It is structured to raise capital in USD as a [dutch auction](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/d/dutchauction.asp), which will last up to six-months––or until the SPEC token supply in the auction is sold out.
The price will decay over the six-month period and participants should place their bids when the price equals their desired value for SPEC/USD.

All accounts will be able to participate. There is no KYC requirement, as the SPEC token is not a security.
SPEC tokens do not guarantee any returns, nor entitle their owners to any future rewards of any kind from the Society Protocol DAO or Society Protocol Foundation.

The ICO is an important step for us to raise the capital required to continue to pursue this world-changing project.
It is a one-time event, designed to distribute SPEC tokens to a diverse worldwide community of supporters for our movement.

We thank all of you for your support.
