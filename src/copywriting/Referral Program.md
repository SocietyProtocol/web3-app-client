# Referral Program

Grow the network by inviting trusted members. Every accepted invitation strengthens the Society Protocol community and unlocks rewards for both you and the people you bring in.

## How it works

The referral program connects you directly to the people you invite, forming a verifiable on-chain relationship between profiles. When you share your referral code with someone new, you vouch for them — and once they accept your invitation, the connection becomes part of your shared network history.

Each profile can generate referral codes tied to its associated wallet address. New users entering the network with a valid code complete their onboarding faster and become formally affiliated with the referrer. Affiliations are recorded on-chain and surface across the protocol: in your profile, in community memberships, and in governance contexts where trust networks matter.

The program is open to all profile holders. There is no limit on how many people you can invite, and accepting a referral does not commit you to anything beyond joining the network — you remain free to participate in any community or hold any badge.

## Generate a referral code

From your profile, paste the wallet address of the person you want to invite into the **Generate a Referral Code** field, then click **Generate**. Share the resulting code with the recipient through any channel.

## Accept an invitation

If someone sent you a referral code, paste it into the **Enter Referral Code to Accept Invitation** field on your profile and click **Accept Invitation**. The invitation is processed on-chain and your affiliation becomes active immediately.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Do I need to be a profile holder to use the referral program?</strong></summary>

Yes. You need an active Society Protocol profile to generate or accept referral codes. If you don't have one yet, mint your profile badge first.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I invite multiple people?</strong></summary>

Yes. Each profile can generate as many referral codes as needed. Every code is tied to a specific recipient address.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can a referral be reversed or removed?</strong></summary>

On-chain affiliations are permanent records. They cannot be undone, so make sure you trust the person you're inviting before generating a code.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What do I get for referring someone?</strong></summary>

Referrals contribute to your reputation and network footprint within the protocol. Specific rewards tied to referrals may be announced as the program evolves.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I accept more than one referral code?</strong></summary>

No. Each profile can be affiliated with one referrer. Once you accept an invitation, the affiliation is locked.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is the referrer responsible for what the referred user does?</strong></summary>

No. Referrals are relationship signals, not custody or financial guarantees. Each user remains responsible for their own actions on the protocol.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Where can I see my referrals?</strong></summary>

Your profile displays affiliations connected to your address. You can view who referred you and who you've referred from there.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Do referral codes expire?</strong></summary>

Referral codes remain valid until they're accepted. If you generated a code for someone who never used it, you can simply share it again or generate a new one for a different address.

</details>
