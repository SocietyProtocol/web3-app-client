# Badges FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>What is a Badge?</strong></summary>

A Badge is an on-chain credential that represents your role, status, or membership within Society Protocol. Badges are non-transferable by default and act as verifiable signals of identity and participation across the network.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What types of Badges exist?</strong></summary>

There are three main types:

- **Official** — issued by Society Protocol (e.g. Core Team, Governor, Advisor, Contributor, Moderator, VIP).
- **Community** — issued by a specific community to its managers, assistants, or members.
- **Individual** — created by a user without being tied to an official role or community.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I know if a Badge is Official?</strong></summary>

Official badges are visually distinct and verified on-chain. Non-Official badges (Community and Individual) are marked with a red border to clearly differentiate them.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Who can issue a Badge?</strong></summary>

Society Protocol issues Official badges. Community managers issue Community badges to their members. Any profile holder can create Individual badges.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I lose a Badge?</strong></summary>

Yes. Badges can be burned by their managers, revoked when leaving a community, or transferred if the badge configuration allows it. Check each badge's permissions to see how it behaves.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can a Badge be transferred?</strong></summary>

It depends on the badge configuration. Each badge defines who can mint, burn, and transfer it. Most identity-related badges are non-transferable.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Where can I see my Badges?</strong></summary>

All your badges appear in your profile under the Badges section. You can also browse the full badge directory in the Badges page.

</details>
